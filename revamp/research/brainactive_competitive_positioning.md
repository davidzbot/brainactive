# BrainActive — Competitive & Product Positioning

## 1. Category map (what else is out there)

| Category | Examples | What they do | Gap BrainActive fills |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Generic brain-training** | Lumosity, Elevate, Peak, Brain Test, Impulse, NeuroNation | Broad cognitive games, Western, age-agnostic, ad/sub driven | Not Singapore-specific, not P3-anchored, no curriculum. We can't beat them on volume; we beat them on **focus + local relevance**. |
| **GEP / high-ability tuition** | Think Academy, Terry Chew, Joyous Learning, Learning Lodge | Courses, worksheets, holiday bootcamps | They sell *tuition*, not a daily game-like app; often drill-heavy. We sell a **10-min daily habit + teaching explanations**. |
| **P3 maths / PSLE apps** | MathHero, PSLE apps, many drill apps | Syllabus practice, word problems | Syllabus-bound. We are **non-routine reasoning**, not drilling. (We can cross-sell with MathHero's existing audience.) |
| **IQ-test / aptitude apps** | Generic IQ, "cognitive assessment" apps | One-off tests, scores | We frame as **skill-building with progress**, not a test or a label. |
| **Local reasoning workbooks** | SAP "Conquer Thinking Skills", FAN-Math, onSponge | Paper heuristics practice | No app, no adaptive daily challenge, no deterministic visual engine. |

## 2. What BrainActive can uniquely own

**"Singapore Primary 3 High-Ability Thinking Practice"** — a daily, game-like app that builds the
reasoning skills MOE's 2026 identification and E2K value (pattern, logic, spatial, heuristics),
with explanations that *teach*, and a visual engine that scales to hundreds of items without
hand-drawn art.

Defensible because:
- **Niche precision:** "P3 high-ability, Singapore" is a high-intent, lower-competition sliver vs "brain games" (huge, Western).
- **Parent willingness to pay:** high-ability / HAL / E2K parents already pay for enrichment; an app is a cheap daily supplement.
- **Asset leverage:** reuses MathHero/PSLE web ecosystem for discovery; deterministic `visual_spec` renderer keeps content cost low at scale.
- **Non-school differentiation:** pattern/visual/logic are exactly the skills *not* taught in primary school — our value is clearest there.

## 3. Positioning statement (recommended)
> **BrainActive — Build Thinking Skills for Primary 3 High Ability.**
> A 10-minute daily brain challenge that trains the reasoning and problem-solving skills
> high-ability learners need — through fun, game-like puzzles with explanations that teach.
> *Not* a test, and not affiliated with MOE or GEP.

## 4. SEO / parent-intent implications

Reliable Singapore-specific search volumes are **not publicly available** to us; we do not assert
volume figures. Instead we map *intent* (from the research and the existing MathHero/PSLE web
footprint):

| Search intent | Example queries | Type | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **High intent (parents ready to act)** | "GEP preparation", "P3 high ability", "high ability learner Singapore", "E2K Mathematics", "GEP questions" | High | Pillar landing pages; capture via existing PSLEHero/MathHero site cross-links. |
| **Mid intent (learning what it is)** | "P3 reasoning", "P3 logic questions", "verbal reasoning P3", "non verbal reasoning P3", "spatial reasoning P3", "primary reasoning", "gifted education Singapore" | Informational | Content cluster / blog; explain skills, link to app. |
| **Brand/feature** | "daily brain challenge", "thinking skills primary 3" | Branded | App store + site. |

**Recommended content architecture (web):**
- Pillars: `/p3-high-ability`, `/verbal-reasoning-p3`, `/non-verbal-reasoning-p3`, `/spatial-reasoning-p3`, `/problem-solving-heuristics-p3`.
- Cluster articles explaining each skill with 1–2 sample puzzles (linking to the app).
- Leverage **MathHero/PSLEHero** existing rankings: interlink "high-ability thinking" as an
  enrichment upsell to maths-seeking parents.
- **Store listing** keywords (from earlier ASO research): "brain games", "memory games",
  "brain training" for discovery, but the *subtitle/description* should lead with
  "P3 high-ability thinking skills / reasoning practice" to attract the right parent.

**Caution:** avoid "GEP test / GEP exam prep" claims; use "high-ability thinking practice" and
"in the spirit of reasoning skills valued by MOE's identification and E2K".

## 5. One-line differentiation
Generic brain apps train *anyone's* brain. Tuition centres drill for *exams*. **BrainActive trains
the specific reasoning habits of Singapore's high-ability P3 children — daily, game-like, and
explained — at app price, not tuition price.**
